Concept 01

Showroom Noir

Dealership-quiet, hairline gold

The page looks like the website of a hypercar maker. Almost-black navy, enormous thin capitals, and gold used only as hairlines and prices. Photography does all the talking.

Palette

Type

Jost 200–500 for everything. Headlines in widely tracked capitals, labels in tiny 11px tracking.

Signature moments

  • Full-bleed hero with a slow Ken-Burns zoom
  • Gold hairline draws across the screen as you scroll
  • Services shown as numbered columns, not cards
  • Prices appear in gold only on hover

Concept 02

Gold Hairline Editorial

A luxury magazine, not a website

Palette

Type

Signature moments
